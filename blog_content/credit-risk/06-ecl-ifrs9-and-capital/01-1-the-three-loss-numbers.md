# §1 · THE THREE LOSS NUMBERS

At any reporting date after April 2027, a bank carries three different loss figures on the same portfolio. Document 05 §7.2 introduced them; here is the map.

| | IRAC provision | ECL | Capital / RWA |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Question** | What do the rules require? | What do we expect to lose? | What could we lose unexpectedly? |
| **Basis** | Asset classification ladder | PD × LGD × EAD, discounted, probability-weighted | Exposure × risk weight |
| **Horizon** | — | 12-month or lifetime by stage | 12-month notionally |
| **PD flavour** | — | Point-in-time, forward-looking | Through-the-cycle in principle; not used at all under SA |
| **Sits in** | P&L and balance sheet | P&L and balance sheet | Capital adequacy computation |
| **From 01-04-2027** | Superseded for banks by ECL | **ACPIR Directions, 2026** | **Capital Charge Directions, 2026** |

📘 **The distinction that keeps this straight, and it is worth saying out loud until it is automatic.** Expected loss is a **cost of doing business** — you price for it and you provision for it. Unexpected loss is a **risk** — you cannot price for it because you do not know when it comes, so you hold capital against it. Provisions absorb the average; capital absorbs the surprise.

⚠️ **The India-specific fact underneath all of §7**, and it is the single most important structural thing to know about Indian bank capital: **the RBI has implemented only the Standardised Approach for credit risk.** There is no Internal Ratings Based approach for Indian banks. Global regulators — the PRA, MAS, HKMA — allow a choice between SA and IRB; India does not.

🔴 The consequence, which is a good answer whenever the IRB formula comes up: **your PD and LGD models do not touch your regulatory capital.** They determine provisions, pricing and internal decisions. Capital comes from a prescribed risk-weight table applied to exposures. So the elaborate parameter apparatus of Document 05 feeds ECL and management, not RWA — which makes the prudential floors of §5 the only place where the regulator constrains parameter quality with a hard number.
