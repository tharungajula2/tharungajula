## HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

Document 05 built PD, LGD and EAD. This document spends them — twice, in two different arithmetics, for two different purposes.

It is the most **dated** document in the set. Almost everything here turns on two instruments issued on the same day, **27 April 2026**, both effective **1 April 2027**. That means two things for you. First, this is the highest-value material in the set for interviews right now, because it is the live project inside every Indian bank and almost nobody outside those projects has the detail. Second, it will age. Read the tags.

The structure: §1 orients you across three loss numbers that coexist. §2 to §6 are the ECL framework. §7 is capital. §8 is how they interact — which is the part people miss and the part that most rewards understanding.

> **The one sentence that organises this document**
>
> Provisions answer *what do we expect to lose?* Capital answers *what could we lose that we did not expect?* — and on 1 April 2027 India rewrites both, on the same day, for the same book.
