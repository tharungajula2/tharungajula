# §9 · WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE WORK

The practical programme inside an Indian bank between now and April 2027, which is also a good answer to "what would you be doing if we hired you into this."

**Data.** Retain and retrieve **origination-date PD** for every live account — without it, quantitative SICR is impossible (§2.3). Build the **transactor ledger** (§7.3). Establish a reliable **borrower-level housing loan count from the bureau** (§7.4). Reconcile product tagging to the level at which prudential floors apply (§5).

**Models.** PD term structures, not just 12-month PDs (Document 05 §4.4). LGD on discounted, cost-net, completed workouts with truncation handled (Document 05 §5). CCF by utilisation band (Document 05 §6.3). Macro scenario models with documented, defensible weights (§3).

**Staging.** Design and calibrate the SICR thresholds — relative, absolute, or both — and quantify the Stage 2 population at each candidate setting. This single calibration will move the provision more than any parameter refinement.

**Governance.** Model inventory, risk-based tiering, independent validation, board subcommittee — per the ECL Directions and the June 2026 draft model risk guidance whose finalisation remains unconfirmed (Document 04's opening).

**Disclosure.** Reconciliation of loss allowances, staging movement, scenario assumptions, overlays quantified separately.

✅ **And the honest framing to offer:** the modelling is the visible part and the smaller part. **The binding constraint on almost every Indian ECL implementation is data — specifically, historical data that was never retained because nothing previously required it.** Origination PDs, complete workout histories, limit-change histories, borrower-level system-wide exposure. A bank that starts building models before it has confronted its data gap will rebuild.
