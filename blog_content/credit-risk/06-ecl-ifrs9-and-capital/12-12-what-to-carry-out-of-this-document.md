# §12 · WHAT TO CARRY OUT OF THIS DOCUMENT

1. **Three loss numbers coexist** — IRAC, ECL, capital — and you should be able to explain every difference between them.
2. **India is SA-only.** Your PD and LGD models drive provisions and pricing, not regulatory capital.
3. **SICR is relative to origination**, so origination PDs must be retained forever. This is the single most common data gap.
4. **The Stage 1 to Stage 2 cliff is roughly 2.7× and larger for long tenor** — so staging drives provision volatility more than parameters do.
5. **Losses are convex in the macro variable**, so run the scenarios, not the average of the scenarios. ₹121 crore against ₹100 crore.
6. **Two glide paths, one year apart** — EIR to 2030, provisioning smoothing to 2031.
7. **In your best segments the floor binds and the model is decorative** — 5.7× in the worked mortgage case.
8. **The transition smooths day one, not variance.** After April 2027 provisions are permanently model-driven and volatile.
9. **Housing risk weight now depends on a bureau lookup**, and cards on a twelve-month behavioural ledger. **Capital has become a measurement problem.**
10. **A provision costs roughly fourteen times more CET1 than the RWA relief it generates.**
