# §11 · THE [VERIFY] LIST

**Closed or confirmed by this document:**

- ✅ **ECL scenario and EIR provisions** — multiple scenarios with bank-determined probability weights under board governance; EIR at initial recognition for post-April-2027 instruments; credit-adjusted EIR for POCI; contractual rate permitted transitionally for opening ECL; full EIR migration by **31 March 2030**.
- ✅ **The two glide paths**, distinctly: EIR to 31 March 2030, provisioning and capital impact smoothing to 31 March 2031.
- ✅ **Housing risk weights** now a function of LTV **and** system-wide housing loan count — 20–40% for the first two, 30–60% from the third, +5pp at ₹3 crore and above; residential real estate NPAs fixed at 100%.
