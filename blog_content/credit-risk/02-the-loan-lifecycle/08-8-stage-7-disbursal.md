# §8 · STAGE 7 — DISBURSAL

Short stage, three real controls.

**1. Where the money goes.** Under the Digital Lending Directions, all flows must move **directly between the lender's account and the borrower's account** — no pooling through an LSP. In secured lending, disbursal typically goes to the seller or dealer rather than the borrower, which is itself a fraud control: it makes an invented transaction harder.

**2. End-use.** Monitored where the loan carries an end-use condition. Note Document 01 §2.2: under the gold directions, end-use monitoring is compulsory **only** where the lender claims priority-sector classification — a deliberate reduction in friction for small-ticket gold borrowers.

**3. Tranching.** Construction-linked disbursal in housing, where money is released against verified project progress. This converts a single credit decision into a series of them, and the failure mode is disbursing against progress that has not occurred.

⚠️ **The first-payment default connection.** The gap between disbursal and the first EMI is where first-party fraud reveals itself. FPD and EPD rates on a fresh cohort are the fastest read you have on whether a sourcing channel has been compromised — available within one or two months of origination, against six to twelve months for a meaningful vintage curve.
