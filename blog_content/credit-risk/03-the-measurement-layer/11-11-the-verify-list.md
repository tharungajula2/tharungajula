# §11 · THE [VERIFY] LIST

**Closed by this document:**

- ✅ **ECL framework** — RBI (Commercial Banks – Asset Classification, Provisioning and Income Recognition) Directions, 2026, RBI/DOR/2026-27/398, dated 27 April 2026, effective 1 April 2027.
- ✅ **Basel III Master Circular** — superseded from 1 April 2027 by the RBI (Commercial Banks – Capital Charge for Credit Risk – Standardised Approach) Directions, 2026, issued the same day.
- ✅ **The 125% / 150% risk weights** — stand until 31 March 2027; restructured from 1 April 2027, with transactor card receivables at 75% and revolvers at 125%.

- The exact current name of the **NBFC Ind AS prudential floor** circular after consolidation.

- **KYC periodic-updation backstop of 30 June 2026** — expired, consequence unconfirmed.
- **ULI** rollout scale; **RDCL** issuance volumes; **SARFAESI** thresholds for NBFCs and HFCs.

**Opened by this document:**

- **The product-wise prudential floor percentages** for Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 under the 2026 ACPIR Directions. Widely summarised; read paragraph-level from the Directions before quoting a number.
- **The precise regulatory retail qualifying criteria** under the new capital directions — the granularity criterion in particular, which I have not confirmed. Turnover threshold reported at ₹500 crore and exposure cap at ₹10 crore; verify.
- **The revised home loan and LAP risk-weight bands** by ticket size.
