# §13 · WHAT TO CARRY OUT OF THIS DOCUMENT

1. **Three modes — Run, Build, Answer.** Automate Run so you have hours for Answer, because Answer is where reputation is made.
2. **Monthly snapshots or nothing.** Without point-in-time history there is no vintage curve, no transition matrix, no backtest, no PSI — and it cannot be reconstructed later.
3. **Reconcile to the general ledger before you publish.** Once.
4. **The five numbers**, and none of them is a stock number.
5. **The room wants a decision, not an analysis** — and the sentence that lands is the one in rupees.
6. **The investigation order: artefact, numerator, cohort-or-flow, segment, shape, front end, quantify.** Never start with segment cuts.
7. **MOB 2 is sourcing or fraud. MOB 6 is credit.** This determines who owns the problem.
8. **The aggregate is the alarm, not the finding.**
9. **Write your own limitations. Test your own implementation.**
10. **Translate, don't simplify** — the consequence, not the mechanism.
11. **In the first ninety days: map, data, reproduce, one finding.** Do not build a model in month one.
