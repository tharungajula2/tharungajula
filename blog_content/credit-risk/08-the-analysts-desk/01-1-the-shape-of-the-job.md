# §1 · THE SHAPE OF THE JOB

## 1.1 Three modes, and they compete for the same hours

**Run.** The recurring machine. Monthly packs, regulatory returns, model monitoring, provision computation. Non-negotiable deadlines, and it will consume as much time as you let it.

**Build.** Models, scorecards, new segmentations, the ECL implementation. High-value, and always the first thing sacrificed when Run overruns.

**Answer.** The ad hoc question. *"Why is Bihar personal loan delinquency up?"* *"What happens to RWA if we grow the gold book by 30%?"* Unpredictable, usually urgent, and — this is the part people miss — **this is where your reputation is actually made.** The monthly pack is expected; the fast, correct, well-framed answer to a hard question is what gets you into the next conversation.

⚠️ **The failure mode of every junior analyst is letting Run consume everything**, then reporting at appraisal time that they produced the pack on time for twelve months. That is a description of a process, not of a contribution. **Automate Run ruthlessly so you have hours for Answer** — and this, concretely, is where your technical background converts into career advantage in a way that a pure-domain colleague cannot match.

## 1.2 The rhythm

| Cadence | Work |
|:--|:--|
| **Daily** | Bounce and mandate failure tracking; collections dashboard refresh |
| **Weekly** | Bucket balances, flow, collections productivity; escalations |
| **Monthly** | The portfolio pack; transition matrices; model monitoring — PSI and CSI; provision computation; regulatory returns |
| **Quarterly** | Vintage refresh; static pool loss curves; portfolio review meeting; board risk committee material; Pillar 3 disclosures |
| **Annually** | Model validation; segmentation review; ICAAP; policy review; scorecard redevelopment decisions |

📘 The ordering principle from Document 03 §9 holds: **frequency follows decision latency, not data availability.** Just because a number can be produced daily does not mean it should be looked at daily. A daily vintage curve is noise wearing the costume of diligence.
