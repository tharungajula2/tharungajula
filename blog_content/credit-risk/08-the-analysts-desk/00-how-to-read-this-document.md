## HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

Documents 01 to 07 built the knowledge. This one is the job.

It exists because the gap between knowing credit risk and being useful in a credit risk team is almost entirely **craft** — knowing which cut to run first, knowing that your number has to tie to finance's number, knowing that a Gini improvement means nothing to a business head and a swap set means everything. None of that is in a textbook, and all of it is visible within a week to anyone already doing the job.

Read it differently from the others. Documents 03 to 06 you read to be able to reason. **This one you read to be able to behave** — to walk into a portfolio review and know what is expected of you.

> **The one sentence that organises this document**
>
> Nobody in the room wants your analysis. They want a **decision** they can defend — and your job is to compress a week of work into the three sentences that change what someone does on Monday.
