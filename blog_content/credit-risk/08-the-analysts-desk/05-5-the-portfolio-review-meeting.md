# §5 · THE PORTFOLIO REVIEW MEETING

## 5.1 Who is in the room and what they want

| Who | Wants |
|:--|:--|
| **Business head** | To keep growing. Will contest any finding that constrains volume |
| **CRO / risk head** | To know what is deteriorating and whether it is being addressed |
| **Finance** | Whether the provision number is going to move, and when |
| **Collections head** | Whether the incoming flow is manageable with current capacity |
| **You** | To make one finding land |

📘 **The room does not want your analysis; it wants a decision.** Your week of work exists to produce a recommendation someone can act on or reject. Present the work rather than the conclusion and you will be asked, politely, what you think — which means you wasted the first ten minutes.

## 5.2 The structure of a finding

Five sentences, in this order. Deviating from the order is the commonest presentational failure.

1. **What changed.** *"Personal loan PAR 31–90 rose from 1.9% to 2.4% this quarter."*
2. **How much it matters, in money.** *"On the terminal roll, that implies about ₹190 crore of additional annual NPA formation."*
3. **Why.** *"It is concentrated in one sourcing partner, in the October and November cohorts, and it appears at months-on-book two and three."*
4. **What that means.** *"Early-MOB concentration in a single channel points to sourcing quality or fraud, not to credit conditions."*
5. **What you recommend, and what you need.** *"Suspend new business from that partner pending an FPD review; I need three weeks and access to the application images."*

🔴 **Sentence two is the one people skip, and it is the one that gets you heard.** A movement from 1.9% to 2.4% sounds small to anyone who does not work in this. **₹190 crore does not.** Document 03 §4.4 exists to let you make that translation, and translating it is the difference between a finding that is noted and a finding that is acted on.

## 5.3 The "so what" test

Before any slide goes in the pack, ask: **if this number moved the other way, would anyone do anything differently?**

If no, it is context, not a finding. Context belongs in an appendix. **A pack full of numbers that nobody would act on trains the room to stop reading the pack**, and then the one number that mattered gets missed too.

## 5.4 Handling the pushback

The business head will contest. Three arguments recur, and each has a correct response.

**"It's just seasoning."** → *You have the vintage curves. Show the same MOB, cohort against cohort.* Document 03 §3.3.

**"The book is growing, so the ratio is misleading."** → *Correct, and it is misleading in the opposite direction.* Growth **dilutes** delinquency (Document 03 §1). If the ratio has risen despite dilution, the underlying deterioration is worse than the ratio shows.

**"Collections just need to work harder."** → *Show where the deterioration sits in months on book.* If it is at MOB 2, collections did not cause it and cannot fix it. Document 02 §1's upstream principle.

⚠️ **And the response to have ready when you are wrong**, because you will be: say so immediately, say what you got wrong and why, and say what you will check differently. **An analyst who defends a wrong number loses the room permanently.** An analyst who corrects themselves quickly gains standing, because the room learns your numbers are reliable in the way that matters — not never wrong, but never defended past the evidence.
