## HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

Document 00 gave you the map and the sourcing discipline. This document gives you the **territory** — the actual book of retail credit in India, product by product, lender by lender, with the numbers a risk person is expected to know cold.

The tags from Document 00 apply throughout:

Callouts follow the same six types as the rest of the set: 📘 notation, 🔴 trap, ✅ check, ⚠️ caution, 🧮 worked example, and **► SAY THIS** — the sentence you actually say out loud when asked.

> **The one sentence that organises this document**
>
> There is no such thing as "the Indian retail book." There are **seven products with almost nothing in common**, held by **five kinds of institution with different capital costs**, sourced through **four channels with different fraud profiles** — and the only reason they get discussed as one thing is that they happen to sit under the same head of a balance sheet.

Every mistake in retail credit risk analysis starts with treating that aggregate as if it means something.
