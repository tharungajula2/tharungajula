# §10 · THE [VERIFY] LIST

## 10.1 Resolved from Document 00

✅ **The Credit Information Reporting amendments are live.** Final amendment directions issued 4 December 2025; deferred from 1 April 2026 and **in force from 1 July 2026**, with reference dates on the 9th, 16th, 23rd and last day of each month. This was the first of Document 00's four open items and it is now closed. `[IN FORCE]`

## 10.2 Still open from Document 00

- The exact current name of the **NBFC Ind AS prudential floor** circular after consolidation.
- Whether the **Basel III Master Circular** survived as a Master Circular or was reissued as Directions.
- The **applicability clause of the 2026 ECL Directions**, read directly rather than through secondary summaries.

## 10.3 New, opened by this document

- **The 125% consumer credit risk weight and the 150% bank credit card weight.** I find no rollback as at 29 July 2026, but this is a live policy question and the exclusion list is what drives the whole growth composition. Check the current Basel III Master Direction.
- **ULI national rollout scale.** No verified participating-lender count or disbursement volume. §7.4.
- **Cards in circulation.** CRIF's 110.2 million against RBI-sourced 118.6 million at the same date. Definitional, but know which you are quoting. §2.5.
- **RDCL issuance volumes** since March 2025. Cited as structurally important; its actual scale is unverified. §2.1.
- **The current RBI sectoral deployment figures for housing**, read from the release rather than from commentary. §1.3.
