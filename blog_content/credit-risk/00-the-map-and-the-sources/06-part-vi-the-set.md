### What this set assumes and what it doesn't

**Assumes** you know what PD, LGD and EAD are; what a scorecard is; what an NPA is; and basic statistics. **This set does not re-teach fundamentals.**

**Does not assume** you know the 2026 Directions, the October 2025 consolidation, the current SA taxonomy, or Indian market structure in operating detail.

**Deliberately compressed:** the IRB risk-weight derivation, corporate and wholesale credit risk, market and operational risk, and treasury. India is SA-only for credit risk and your niche is retail. Depth goes where the work is.

**Deliberately deep:** unsecured retail — personal loans and credit cards — because that is where the stress, the regulatory attention and the hiring are.
