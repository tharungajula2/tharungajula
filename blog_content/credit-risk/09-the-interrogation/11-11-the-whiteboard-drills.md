# §11 · THE WHITEBOARD DRILLS

Things you should be able to produce on paper, unprompted, in under two minutes each.

1. **The SMA-to-NPA calendar** from a stated due date.
2. **A WoE and IV table** for five bins, computed from raw good/bad counts.
3. **The scaling formulas**, and Factor and Offset from a stated base score, base odds and PDO.
4. **A five-state monthly transition matrix**, and the terminal roll from it.
5. **The chain from terminal roll to annualised NPA formation in rupees.**
6. **A PD term structure table** — marginal, survivors, cumulative — over five years.
7. **An LGD computation** with discounting and costs, alongside the naive version.
8. **A realised CCF** from limit, drawn-at-observation and drawn-at-default.
9. **Probability-weighted ECL** across three scenarios, with the convexity point.
10. **India's capital stack**, and an effective CET1 requirement for a named D-SIB.
11. **A RAROC comparison** of two products at different risk weights.
12. **The eight-table retail credit data model.**
