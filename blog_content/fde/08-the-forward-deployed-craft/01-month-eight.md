## Month eight

Here is what you have. A system processing 40,000 files a month at 0.93 discrepancy catch rate. A monitoring plan agreed with model risk. A security posture Rina signed. A fairness finding you surfaced yourself. An underwriter who edits 19% of memos and rejects 5%.

Here is what you don't have. Any evidence that Meridian's engineers can extend this without you. Any idea whether the other thirty-eight underwriters use it the way Tom does. Any written account of the twenty judgement calls that shaped the architecture. And nothing — nothing at all — going back to your own company about what this engagement taught that would make the next one faster.

**All four of those are your job**, and the technical curriculum you've just spent seven documents on never mentions any of them.

This document is the other half. It's the half that separates <cite index="32-1">an FDE from a consultant who stayed longer</cite>, and it's where the compounding is.
