## What this set was

Eight documents, one deployment, start to finish.

You met Meridian in a room where the slide said "AI-Powered Underwriting Transformation" and nobody had watched an underwriter work. You found the real bottleneck by sitting next to Tom Beaudry for two days. You scoped it down to something shippable and defensible. You built retrieval that could find a discrepancy buried on page 180 of a scanned tax transcript, an evaluation apparatus that pointed at its own failures, a security architecture that made catastrophe structurally impossible rather than merely unlikely, and a fairness test that found a problem and cost you a fifth of the volume.

Then you deployed it into someone else's cloud, through someone else's identity provider, past someone else's change board — and left it with people who could fix it without you.

**The technical content of this set is the smaller half.** The larger half is the habit underneath all of it: **find out what's actually true, scope to what you can defend, measure your own failures, and build so that the dangerous thing is impossible rather than discouraged.**

That habit transfers to every deployment, every domain, and every model generation. The frontier will move. Meridian's file formats will change. The version numbers in Document 01 are already stale.

**The habit isn't.**
