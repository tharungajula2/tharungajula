## PART VII — THE SET

- **00 — THE ROLE AND THE MAP** *(this document)*
- **01 — THE FLOOR.** How models actually work, and the engineer's craft underneath everything: Python that survives review, async, Pydantic, Git, the terminal. Discovery at Meridian begins. You find the real bottleneck.
- **02 — THE INSTRUMENT.** Prompting, APIs, structured output, tool calling, streaming, retries, cost control. The scoping conversation and the first demo.
- **03 — THE CUSTOMER'S DATA.** Retrieval: chunking, embeddings, hybrid search, reranking, citations, ingestion pipelines, failure modes. Integration archaeology on Meridian's loan files. The single most common FDE deployment shape in existence.
- **04 — THE LOOP.** Agents, ReAct, LangGraph, human-in-the-loop gates, multi-agent patterns, MCP, memory. When the summariser needs to fetch, verify, and reason across multiple sources.
- **05 — THE PROOF.** Golden datasets, LLM-as-judge, trajectory evals, tracing, observability, CI gates. Marcus arrives. This document is how you survive him.
- **06 — THE HOSTILE WORLD.** Prompt injection, the lethal trifecta, least-privilege tools, PII, proxy discrimination, compliance architecture. Rina and Dan. This is where the deployment is genuinely won or lost.
- **07 — SHIPPING IT.** FastAPI, Docker, Postgres, queues, OAuth, secrets, monitoring, SLOs, multi-tenancy, rollback drills. The 80%. The largest document in the set, by design.
- **08 — THE FORWARD-DEPLOYED CRAFT.** Entirely new material: discovery technique, scoping and saying no, demo craft, enterprise auth reality, pilot-to-production conversion, adoption, handover, stakeholder management, pattern-back-to-product. Plus frontier awareness and the full receipts index.
- **09 — THE INTERROGATION.** Every self-test, pulled out of the notes and gathered in one place, so the notes stay pure narrative.
