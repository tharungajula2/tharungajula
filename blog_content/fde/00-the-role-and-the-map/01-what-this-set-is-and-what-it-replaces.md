## What this set is, and what it replaces

You own ten volumes of AI-engineering material — 228 chapters, roughly 177,000 words. It is good material. It is also organised like a syllabus: foundations, then prompting, then retrieval, then agents, and so on outward. That ordering is honest for a curriculum and useless for a job, because no job arrives in that order. A job arrives as one customer with one broken process and a deadline.

So this set does something different. It takes the technical substance of those ten volumes, cuts what a Forward Deployed Engineer will never touch, deepens what an FDE lives inside daily, adds the entire customer-facing half that the textbooks never covered — and reorders all of it along the arc of **one real deployment, start to finish**.

You are not studying nine documents. You are shipping one system, and learning everything at the moment the system demands it.

There are nine documents plus this map. Read them in order. Each one picks up where the last put the deployment down.
