## What was cut, and why

Honesty about the edges. Deliberately reduced to awareness-level: fine-tuning internals, LoRA and QLoRA mechanics, local model hosting, GraphRAG frontier techniques, most reasoning-research material, and the paper-reading discipline. Roughly Volume 10's Parts I and II.

Not because they are unimportant. Because an FDE's job with these is to **recognise them and know when to say no** — and the correct answer is almost always no. When a customer asks "should we fine-tune?", the professional response is a cost-and-maintenance argument for prompting or retrieval instead, delivered in ninety seconds. You need to be able to give that argument. You do not need to be able to train the adapter.

Everything cut is preserved in your original volumes. Nothing is lost. It is deprioritised, which is a different thing, and it is the same judgement call an FDE makes about scope every single week.
