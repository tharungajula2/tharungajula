## The meeting you actually wanted

You're in a room with Marcus and two people from his validation team. There is no demo. Nobody wants to see the system work.

They want to know: **what number tells you it's broken, and who sees that number when it moves.**

This is a better meeting than the demo, and here's why. Every vendor Meridian has ever bought from could demonstrate capability. Almost none of them could hand over an instrument. The team that arrives with a measuring apparatus — one that points at its own failures, that a third party can run, whose results are reproducible six months later — is in a different category, and validation functions can tell the difference in about ten minutes.

So this document is not "how to test AI." It is **how to build the instrument you hand to the person whose job is to distrust you**, and how to keep that instrument honest.
