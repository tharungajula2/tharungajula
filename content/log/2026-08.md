### 1. Mathematical Formulation & Risk Metrics

In retail credit risk modelling, Expected Loss ($EL$) is governed by the fundamental credit risk equation:

$$EL = PD \times LGD \times EAD$$

Where:
- **Probability of Default ($PD$):** Logit model parameterization $\text{logit}(p) = \beta_0 + \sum_{i=1}^n \beta_i X_i$
- **Loss Given Default ($LGD$):** Economic loss fraction post-recovery: $LGD = 1 - \text{Recovery Rate}$
- **Exposure at Default ($EAD$):** Total drawn balance plus expected CCF: $EAD = \text{Drawn} + (\text{Undrawn} \times \text{CCF})$
