## Plain definition
Attention is a mathematical mechanism that allows a language model to compute correlations between different words (tokens) in a sentence, calculating dynamically which words are most relevant to one another regardless of their physical distance.

## Why it matters
Attention is the core mathematical engine that powers modern transformers. Instead of processing input step-by-step in order, models evaluate context elements simultaneously, allowing for rich, parallelized comprehension.
