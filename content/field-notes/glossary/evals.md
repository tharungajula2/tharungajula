## Plain definition
Evaluations (evals) are structured, repeatable testing frameworks and datasets designed to measure the quality, accuracy, cost, and latency of a generative AI model's output against target benchmarks.

## Why it matters
Unlike traditional code where outputs are fully deterministic, AI outputs are statistical. Programmatic evaluations are the only way to systematically measure if model updates, prompt changes, or pipeline adjustments actually improve performance.
