The order is not a convenience. Each stage consumes an artefact the previous one produced, and running two of them out of sequence is how leakage enters a scorecard.

C · THE LOSS ENGINE07/100

Expected loss is a price. Unexpected loss is a risk.

Expected loss goes into the interest spread and into provisions. What capital exists for is unexpected loss — the year when realised losses are three times the expected level.

Confusing the two is the most expensive error in the subject: I either under-provide and over-capitalise, or the reverse.

This is why the IRB formula subtracts PD inside its brackets. Expected loss is already funded, so capital must cover only the excess.

And it is why the provision stack and the capital stack are separate numbers on a balance sheet. By the time a loss arrives at its expected size, it has been funded twice.

Where conservatism belongs, and why

Padding PD changes who gets approved

PD drives the cut-off, so inflating it distorts the ranking that sanction decisions are made on. A PD that is deliberately wrong declines borrowers who should have been taken.

Padding LGD raises provisions without touching the ranking

Severity multiplies money after the decision has been taken. So I keep PD as unbiased as the data allows and put conservatism in LGD and EAD.

INDIA. The RBI made the same choice for me. Backstop LGDs of 65% secured and 70% unsecured are deliberately punitive numbers that penalise not knowing — a bank that invests in recovery data uses its own estimates; a bank that does not pays for the ignorance.

D · DEFINING DEFAULT10/100

Default is a defined event, not a natural fact

DEFINE · DEFAULT

The event the model predicts. It must be a rule, applied identically to every row, with a stated observation date and a stated horizon. There is no natural definition — there is only a documented one.

DEFINE · OBSERVATION POINT

The date I freeze everything known about an account. Nothing recorded after it may enter the model.

DEFINE · OUTCOME WINDOW

The fixed period afterward during which I watch for default. Inside the window → bad. Never → good. A model can only predict over the window it was trained on.

Window length is a real trade-off. Twelve months for unsecured personal loans and cards, because it captures most of the emergence and keeps the sample recent. Twenty-four months or more for housing, because those defaults arrive slowly — at the cost of a model built on borrowers from two years ago.

D · DEFINING DEFAULT13/100

Cure — and why LGD exists at all

TRAP · "DEFAULT MEANS LOSS"

It doesn't. Accounts cure: a borrower who reaches 90 days overdue and then clears the arrears has defaulted and produced no loss. On secured retail, cure rates commonly run a third to a half of defaults.

This is the entire reason LGD is modelled rather than assumed to be 1.

The consequence for my own book

My LGD is 93.01% precisely because cure barely happens here. These are unsecured US consumer term loans charged off after 120-plus days, with nothing to enforce against. On a secured Indian book the same mechanism runs the other way and LGD collapses into single or low double digits.

INDIA. Upgradation is all-or-nothing: the entire arrears of interest and principal must be cleared across all facilities. That makes cure a discrete, lumpy event — which is exactly why LGD distributions are bimodal.

CHECK · DEFINITION CONSISTENCY

Run three flags over the same month: the flag used to build the model, the flag used for supervisory NPA reporting, and the flag that will drive Stage 3.

Pass condition: they agree within a few percent, and every disagreement traces to a documented rule.

A model trained on facility-level 90-DPD systematically under-counts defaults against the borrower-level regulatory definition.

What I can now do

THE THREE LIMBS OF "OUT OF ORDER"

1 · Over limit for 90 continuous days   2 · No credits for 90 days   3 · Credits insufficient to cover the interest debited in the prior 90 days.

The third is the subtle one: an account can be inside its limit, receiving money monthly, and still be NPA.

Borrower level, not facility level. One defaulted credit card makes the same customer's housing loan an NPA. Upgradation requires the entire arrears across all facilities.

The ECL framework does not replace NPA classification. Under the 2026 Directions, "default" for ECL purposes is defined as NPA status; a bank reports both a stage and an asset classification for the same account.

E · SAMPLING AND SPLIT DESIGN15/100

A split done badly leaks. A split done well is itself evidence.

DEFINE · OUT-OF-TIME VALIDATION

Testing on a later time period than the one I trained on, rather than a random holdout. A random test split tells me the model generalises across accounts. An OOT sample tells me it generalises across time.

That is the failure mode which actually kills scorecards, because populations and economies drift.

DEFINE · STRATIFIED SPLIT

Random sampling within strata of the target, so the default rate is preserved in every partition.

Without it, a 3.4% event rate can drift materially between splits by chance alone, and the comparison between partitions is contaminated before it starts.

TRAP · "A RANDOM 80/20 SPLIT IS ENOUGH"

It measures the wrong thing. Random holdout answers "does this work on other accounts from the same period?" Every real scorecard failure is "does this still work next year?" Only an OOT sample answers that.

Why the split must be frozen first

The split is fixed before a single feature is examined. WoE bins, feature shortlists and cut-offs are all fitted downstream of it. Fitting anything on the full data before splitting is the quietest way to leak, and it is invisible in the results.

What each partition is asked

Train — fit everything. Test — does it hold on unseen accounts from the same period? OOT — does it hold on a later vintage the model has never seen, drawn from a different origination environment?

The correction I did not need, and the class I did not carve out

DEFINE · OVERSAMPLING AND THE INTERCEPT CORRECTION

Bads are rare, so many shops keep all bads and randomly down-sample goods. This speeds estimation and leaves the slopes untouched, but it shifts the intercept, which must be corrected.

\[ \beta_0^{corrected} = \hat{\beta}_0 - \ln(\rho_1 / \rho_0) \]
ρ₁The sampling fraction on bads — usually 1, because all of them are kept.
ρ₀The sampling fraction on goods — the fraction actually retained.
β̂₀The intercept as fitted on the down-sampled data.

I did not oversample. 184,525 rows with 6,329 events is plenty, so no correction was needed. I am ready to say that unprompted — it is a common probe, and the wrong answer is to look blank.

DEFINE · INDETERMINATES

Accounts neither clearly good nor clearly bad in the window — typically reached 30 or 60 DPD but never defaulted, or closed early for non-credit reasons.

Usually excluded, because counting them as goods teaches the model that near-misses are fine and counting them as bads teaches it that one late payment is fatal.

If they exceed roughly 5–10% of the sample, the exclusion needs written justification and the model needs recalibrating back onto the full population.

ADMIT

I did not carve out an indeterminate class. It is a simplification, and I name it before being asked.

E · SAMPLING AND SPLIT DESIGN18/100

Reject inference, and its only real cure

DEFINE · REJECT INFERENCE

My training sample contains only sanctioned accounts, but the model will be applied to everyone who applies. Techniques such as parcelling — score the rejects, assign bad flags in proportion to inferred odds, usually inflated 2–4× — and augmentation try to fill the gap.

TRAP · "REJECT INFERENCE FIXES SELECTION BIAS"

It cannot. The outcomes were never observed and no algorithm creates data.

What it does is make the assumption about the rejected region explicit, consistent and auditable. That is worth doing. It is not the same as knowing.

The only genuine cure, and it is expensive

Deliberately sanction a random sample of applicants I would otherwise decline, and watch them. Nothing else produces real evidence about the region below the cut-off.

Every other method is an assumption dressed as data.

My data is already an accepted-only book — LendingClub publishes funded loans — so my model inherits that selection, and I say so.

F · WEIGHT OF EVIDENCE AND IV19/100

Almost all the craft is in the transformation, not the regression

Why raw variables go into a logistic regression badly

Income is skewed and partly fictional — self-reported, unverified, and long-tailed.

Age is non-monotonic in risk — the young and the very old are not on the same side of any straight line.

Missing values are informative, not absent. "Declined to state income" is a fact about the applicant.

Outliers drag coefficients — one implausible income moves the fitted line for everyone.

The industry answer is to bin every variable into a handful of ranges, and replace each range with a single number encoding its riskiness.

DEFINE · FINE CLASSING

Cut a variable into ~20 narrow bins and plot the bad rate of each. This is diagnostic: it shows the shape of the relationship before any decision is taken.

DEFINE · COARSE CLASSING

Merge adjacent bins until three conditions hold at once: the pattern is monotonic (or at least defensible), every bin holds at least ~5% of the sample, and adjacent bins differ meaningfully in bad rate.

Missing values usually become their own bin.

The bins are a modelling decision, not preprocessing. They are fitted, they are stored, and they ship with the model.

F · WEIGHT OF EVIDENCE AND IV20/100

Weight of evidence and information value

DEFINE · WEIGHT OF EVIDENCE

The log-odds of a bin relative to the book.

\[ WOE_i = \ln\!\left(\frac{g_i / G}{b_i / B}\right) \]
giGoods in bin i.
biBads in bin i.
G, BTotal goods and total bads in the book.

Positive WoE = proportionally more goods than the book average = safer than average. Some houses put bads on top and flip every sign; either convention works if used throughout.

DEFINE · INFORMATION VALUE

One number summarising how well a variable separates goods from bads, before any other variable is considered.

\[ IV = \sum_i \left(\frac{g_i}{G} - \frac{b_i}{B}\right) \times WOE_i \]

Every term is non-negative, because the proportion difference and the log-ratio always share a sign.

IVVerdict
< 0.02Useless
0.02 – 0.1Weak
0.1 – 0.3Medium
0.3 – 0.5Strong
> 0.5Suspect — usually a consequence of default, not a predictor

The four things WoE buys at once

This is the answer to "why not just one-hot encode?"

1

The relationship becomes linear by construction in log-odds space — which is exactly what logistic regression assumes.

2

Outliers are capped inside their bins. An implausible income sits in the top bin and stops there.

3

Missing values get a coefficient instead of being dropped or imputed.

4

Under the standard convention every fitted coefficient should come out with the same sign — so a sign check becomes a free diagnostic. A wrong-signed coefficient means multicollinearity or a bad bin, and I catch it in one glance.

TRADE-OFF · LOGISTIC REGRESSION VS GRADIENT BOOSTING

A boosted tree ensemble usually beats a well-built scorecard by 2–5 Gini points in development and often much less out of time.

DEFINE · HAZARD RATE

ht = the probability of defaulting in period t, given survival to the start of it. It is a conditional probability, which is what makes it composable across periods.

\[ S_t = \prod_{k \le t}(1 - h_k) \qquad PD_t^{marg} = S_{t-1} \times h_t \]

Survival is the running product of not-defaulting. The marginal PD is the unconditional probability of defaulting in period t — I had to survive to t first, then fail.

Cumulative PD to time T is 1 − ST, and equally the sum of the marginals.

TRAP · "LIFETIME PD IS 12-MONTH PD TIMES THE YEARS"

DEFINE · SICR

Significant Increase in Credit Risk — the Stage 1 → Stage 2 trigger. Critically, it is a change in default risk over the remaining life, not an absolute level of risk.

A borrower who was always risky and stayed equally risky does not trigger SICR. A safe borrower who doubled in risk does — even if they are still safer in absolute terms.

Why the interest treatment changes at Stage 3

In Stages 1 and 2 the instrument is still expected to perform, so interest accrues on the gross amount. At Stage 3 the asset is credit-impaired, so interest is recognised on the net carrying amount and on receipt.

That is the accounting analogue of India's income-recognition rule at NPA: the lender stops booking money it is not going to get.

The cliff effect, and my weakest link

TRAP · "STAGE 2 IS A WARNING; THE MONEY IS ALL IN STAGE 3"

The allowance often more than doubles on migration from Stage 1 to Stage 2 — and on a long-dated product the multiple runs to five or ten times — for an account whose underlying risk moved only modestly.

Why gross NPA ratios lie in a growing book

DEFINE · VINTAGE CURVE

Group accounts by the month they were disbursed (the vintage) and plot cumulative bad rate against months on book, not calendar date. This removes the growth distortion entirely, because no two cohorts are ever mixed.

monitoring/vintage.py produces vintage_curves.csv and vintage_maturity_comparison.csv: cumulative default rate by MOB for each origination year 2007–2014, plus a like-for-like comparison at matched maturity so a young vintage is never compared to a mature one at face value.

The mechanism

The headline ratio divides today's bad balance by today's total advances. In a book growing 25% a year the denominator is fresh and the numerator is not — the accounts capable of being 90 days overdue were disbursed months ago, when the book was smaller.

The ratio falls purely because of growth, and keeps falling until growth stops — at which point it jumps, and the jump gets blamed on the economy.

Two corrections

Lagged delinquency — divide by the balance as it stood some months ago. Or vintage analysis — never mix cohorts.

INDIA adds a third distortion. Technical write-offs remove balances from the numerator without any cash arriving, so gross NPA can fall for two independent cosmetic reasons at once.

Three effects distort almost every headline number

Seasoning — a young book looks good.

Mix — composition drift. If the share of unsecured or digitally-sourced business rises, the portfolio bad rate rises even though no segment's rate changed.

Attrition — good borrowers refinance away when rates fall or a competitor offers a balance transfer, leaving a worse residual population. A book can deteriorate without a single account deteriorating.

Flow rate, cure rate, and the cliff at the end of the ladder

Flow rate is a roll rate read from the perspective of accounts leaving rather than arriving.

Cure rate is the proportion moving back toward standard — in India, clearing the entire arrears across all facilities.

The two are not complements, because accounts can also stay put, close, or be written off.

Provision coverage ratio is provisions ÷ gross NPAs.

Cure rates are the most volatile item in any pack, because they respond to collections staffing, to seasonal payment patterns around harvests and festivals, and to restructuring policy — so an unexplained rise in cure rate is more often an operational change than an improvement in customer health.

The boundary between SMA-2 and NPA is not a step on a ladder but the edge of a cliff, because crossing it changes income recognition, provisioning, capital and the borrower's bureau record simultaneously.

Why roll rates come before everything else in a monitoring pack

They are a flow measure on a flow system. Stocks — gross NPA, provision coverage — describe where the book already is. Roll rates describe where it is going, and they turn first.

Adverse selection and the winner's curse

Raising the rate on a segment does two things

It raises margin on everyone who still takes the loan. And it changes who still takes it — and the second usually dominates.

Borrowers with good alternatives — disproportionately the better credits — go elsewhere. So the observed default rate rises after a price increase even though no individual borrower changed.

The portfolio-level cousin. Across a competitive market a lender systematically wins the business its model under-prices relative to everyone else's. A lender with a weaker model doesn't merely earn less — it acquires a book selected against it.

India permits risk-based pricing and constrains it through transparency rather than rate caps

  • Floating-rate retail loans must be linked to an external benchmark.
  • Every retail borrower gets a Key Facts Statement with the all-in APR.
  • Penal charges must be charges, not penal interest, and may not be capitalised.
  • Floating-rate loans to individuals for non-business purposes carry no foreclosure charges.

Microfinance is the exception, with hard limits — household income ceiling ₹3 lakh and total repayment obligations capped at 50% of monthly household income.

Collections targets the middle, not the tail

Once an account is delinquent the question changes from "will this go wrong" to "which intervention on which account recovers the most cash per hour of effort". It is a resource allocation problem with a hard constraint: more delinquent accounts than agent-hours.

The counterintuitive result: do not call the worst accounts. An account almost certain to self-cure wastes the call; an account almost certain to be lost wastes it too. Value is concentrated where the intervention changes the outcome — the middle of the distribution. A collections score typically predicts probability of self-cure without contact, or expected recovery under each treatment.

DEFINE · THREE THINGS THAT LOOK ALIKE AND ARE NOT

Restructuring — a concession granted because of financial difficulty (moratorium, tenor extension, rate cut, capitalisation of arrears). Not generosity: a bet that a temporarily distressed borrower yields more cash than enforcement. Granting it is itself evidence of impairment, and restructured accounts follow their own upgradation path.

Compromise settlement — the lender accepts less than full dues in full and final settlement, closing the account.

Technical write-off — removes a balance from the books without extinguishing the claim; recovery efforts continue. It flatters gross NPA without any cash arriving, and it must be carried explicitly in any LGD dataset, because a written-off account is not a resolved one.

Both of the first two create the governance risk called extend-and-pretend — a book where arrears look low because delinquency has been repeatedly restructured away. That is why restructured and settled balances are reported separately, and why re-default rates on them are among the most watched numbers in a stressed book.

India — conduct rules

Recovery agents act as the lender's agents and the lender remains responsible for their conduct. Contact restricted to reasonable hours — conventionally 8 a.m.–7 p.m. Intimidation and contacting people outside the agreed list are prohibited.

Digital lending adds: lending service providers must be disclosed, limits may not be increased automatically without consent, and any default loss guarantee is capped at 5% of the underlying portfolio.

A wilful defaulter — dues ₹25 lakh+, means to pay but doesn't, or diverts funds — attracts an additional 5% provision.

Stress testing, model risk and fairness

DEFINE · SATELLITE MODEL

A regression of an observed portfolio quantity — default rate, roll rate, recovery rate — on lagged macro variables. Lags are long: employment deterioration leads unsecured defaults by two to four quarters; property prices lead housing losses by longer, because enforcement takes time.

DEFINE · STRESS TEST

A projection of losses, revenues and capital under a prescribed adverse path, to answer whether the institution stays above its capital minimum throughout. Not a forecast — the scenario is chosen to be severe and improbable. In India the RBI runs system-wide macro stress tests and publishes them; individual banks run their own under ICAAP.

Drivers that matter in Indian retail: real income growth and employment; the policy repo rate feeding the external benchmark, which changes instalments directly on floating-rate books; residential property prices driving housing LGD through the equity cushion; inflation compressing household surplus; and for the rural book, the monsoon and crop prices above all of the above.

Because Indian retail capital comes from fixed risk weights, the stress bites almost entirely through provisions and through migration into the 125% and NPA buckets, not through any recalculation of the weights.

The Indian data problem, stated plainly. The usable stress record is thin, and the largest episode is unusable at face value: the 2020 moratorium and the standstill on asset classification suspended the very ageing process that defines default. Days-past-due series from that period record policy, not borrower behaviour. Any model calibrated across it without explicit adjustment misstates both the level and the timing of stress.

DEFINE · MODEL RISK

Risk of loss from decisions based on incorrect or misused models. Two sources: the model may be wrong, or it may be right and used for something it wasn't built for.

The 2026 Directions prescribe a three-tier structure: model owners in the front line, accountable for approval, validation and performance; risk management and compliance, overseeing independent validation and enforcing limits; internal audit, giving objective assurance on both, reporting to the board or audit committee. Above all three sits a board committee that must include the CFO and CRO.

Three specific obligations where implementations usually fail: a model inventory (every model, owners, tier, dependencies, validation status), risk-based tiering so validation intensity follows materiality, and a documented lifecycle with a prospectus stating methodology, limitations and initial validation outcomes.

Independent validation asks three questions in order. Is the model conceptually sound? Does ongoing monitoring show it still works? Does outcomes analysis show predictions matching reality on unseen data? A model can pass the third and fail the first, and that combination is the dangerous one — it works until the regime it silently depends on changes.

TRAP · "EXCLUDING SENSITIVE ATTRIBUTES MAKES A MODEL FAIR"

It prevents direct use only. A model with enough correlated inputs — postal code, device, employer type, education — reconstructs the omitted variable implicitly, and does so better the more flexible it is. The correction is to test outcomes by group rather than inspect inputs, document business necessity for variables producing an adverse disparity, and look for a less discriminatory alternative of comparable power.

When to retire a model: a sustained fall in discrimination that recalibration can't address; a population shift large enough that the development sample no longer resembles the applicant flow; a change in product or classification rules that breaks the target; and simple age. Most retail scorecards are redeveloped on a two-to-four-year cycle.

The loop, restated — Quantitative Risk Pipeline Summary

I define an event — for me, a 12-month default inferred from last payment date; in India, an NPA at 90 days at borrower level. I freeze a photograph of the borrower and wait a stated period for the verdict, keeping the split honest across time as well as across accounts.

I fit something that ranks — WoE bins into a logistic regression, scaled to points because nobody runs a business on log-odds — and something that levels, which is a separate job with a separate fix.

Then I multiply the resulting probability by a severity and an exposure, and because accounting doesn't stop at twelve months, I stretch the probability into a term structure using hazards and survival.

Then I hand the answer to three consumers who each treat it completely differently.

Pricing wants today's odds.

Provisioning wants today's odds under a forecast, staged by whether risk has moved since origination, and then — in India — compared against a floor that frequently wins.

Capital in India ignores my model entirely and reads a number from a table.

Then the population drifts away from the one I measured, PSI tells me it happened, CSI tells me which variable did it, and I start again.
