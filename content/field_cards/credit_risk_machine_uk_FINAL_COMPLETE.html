Before the map: know what kind of thing you are looking at
The biggest source of confusion is mixing a rule, a bank team, a vendor product, a calculation and a BA artefact. These are different object types.
Regulation / standard

A rulebook or supervisory expectation telling the bank what outcome or control is required.

Examples: IFRS 9, Basel 3.1, BCBS 239
Bank workflow

A business activity performed inside the bank as a credit moves from request to monitoring.

Examples: origination, underwriting, limits
Risk number / calculation

A governed measure computed from data. These are the places where equations genuinely matter.

Examples: PD, LGD, EAD, ECL, RWA
Bank team / governance

People inside the bank who own a decision, process, model, report or independent challenge.

Examples: Credit Risk, Finance, Treasury, Model Validation
Financial market product

A contract or security the bank lends through, invests in, trades, funds with or uses to hedge risk.

Examples: bonds, equities, FX, swaps, repo
Vendor product

External software, data or models the bank may buy. A vendor supports the bank; it does not replace bank accountability.

Examples: CreditLens, EDF-X, ImpairmentStudio
BA / delivery artefact

A specification or evidence object that makes business meaning buildable, testable and traceable.

Examples: BRD, mapping, data dictionary, UAT pack

The one sentence to carry

A bank borrows short, lends long, and must prove it can survive both loss and cash stress.

Credit Risk controls borrower risk. Finance measures accounting losses. Prudential Capital converts risk into capital requirements. Treasury keeps the bank funded and liquid. Reporting submits the numbers. Technology moves the data. The BA makes the meaning consistent and the evidence traceable from rule to production.

Water-scrum-fall — what the delivery model actually means
Water: interpretation, scope and approvals are baselined because the regulatory outcome and evidence matter. Scrum: engineering builds and tests in iterations. Fall: UAT, governance, change approval and production handover are formal gates. It is a practical hybrid, not a regulation.
