The one sentence to carry

A bank borrows short, lends long, and must prove it can survive both loss and cash stress.

Credit risk asks whether borrowers repay. IFRS 9 provisions cover expected losses. Basel/PRA capital covers unexpected losses. Liquidity rules stop a solvent bank dying for lack of cash. Regulatory reporting proves the results. The BA makes the entire chain traceable and testable.

Correct the bank's IFRS 9 staging logic and ship it

A realistic hypothetical change: the current implementation uses a narrow 30-DPD rule. You will take the correction through policy interpretation, data mapping, model governance, build, UAT, Finance sign-off, production release and hypercare.

Key accounting fact: 30 days past due is a rebuttable presumption/backstop for SICR — not the complete definition of Stage 2. Qualitative and forward-looking evidence can move an exposure earlier.
