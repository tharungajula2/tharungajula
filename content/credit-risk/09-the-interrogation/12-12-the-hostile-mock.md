# §12 · THE HOSTILE MOCK

Ten questions in sequence, as a sceptical risk manager would actually ask them. Do this one out loud, standing up, without notes.

1. *"Tell me what's happening in Indian retail credit right now."*
2. *"You said gold grew 50%. Is that a good thing?"*
3. *"Our GNPA is 1.8%. Why should I be worried about anything?"*
4. *"Our personal loan delinquency improved last quarter. Convince me it's real."*
5. *"How would you know this book was turning before the NPA number moved?"*
6. *"We're getting a new scorecard. The vendor says Gini 0.52. Should I buy it?"*
7. *"Explain ECL to me as if I run the business, not the risk function."*
8. *"What breaks in April 2027 that isn't breaking now?"*
9. *"You have thirty seconds with the CEO. What's the one number?"*
10. *"What's the weakest part of our whole risk framework, and don't be diplomatic."*

⚠️ **On question 10.** The right answer for an Indian retail book is almost certainly **LGD**, and the reasoning is Document 05 §5.5: it needs completed workouts, the book grew too fast to have many, excluding incomplete workouts biases toward fast resolutions which are the good ones, and there's no observed downturn in which collateral values fell and recovery data was cleanly captured. **Saying that reads as experience. Claiming everything is robust reads as someone who has only read about it.**
