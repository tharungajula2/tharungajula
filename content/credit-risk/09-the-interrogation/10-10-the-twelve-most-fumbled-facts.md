# §10 · THE TWELVE MOST-FUMBLED FACTS

Work these until they are automatic. Each is a free win and each is commonly got wrong.

1. **A high Hosmer–Lemeshow p-value is a PASS.**
2. **An NPA upgrades only on payment of the ENTIRE arrears** — not on DPD falling below 90, not on partial payment.
3. **SICR is relative to origination**, not an absolute risk level.
4. **Penal CHARGE, not penal interest**, and not capitalised.
5. **V-CIP equals face-to-face; OTP-based e-KYC does not.**
6. **India's minimum CRAR is 9%, not Basel's 8%.**
7. **A buffer is meant to be used**; breaching it restricts distributions, not compliance.
8. **Technical write-off removes the asset, not the liability.**
9. **Growth dilutes delinquency** — 4.0% becomes 2.7% with nothing improved.
10. **The FSR's 8.1% is a sensitivity, not a scenario.**
11. **Lifetime PD is built from declining marginal hazards** — 8.14%, not 15%.
12. **India is Standardised-Approach only** — your PD and LGD don't drive regulatory capital.
