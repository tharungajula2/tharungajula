## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

This is the only document in the set meant to be opened **out of order**.

**The protocol, and it matters more than the content:**

1. **Cover the answer.** Physically, or with your hand on a phone screen. Reading a question and its answer together produces recognition, which feels like knowing and is not.
2. **Say it out loud.** Not in your head. The gap between what you can recognise and what you can *produce under mild pressure with someone watching* is enormous, and speaking is the only way to close it. This is the whole reason this document exists separately.
3. **Fifteen minutes.** Longer and you stop retrieving and start reading.
4. **Mark every miss.** A small dot beside the question. On the third pass, work only the dotted ones.
5. **Space it.** Same section again after two days, then a week, then a fortnight. Massed repetition on one evening produces almost nothing durable.

⚠️ **One honest warning.** Some answers here are three sentences long. In a real room the answer is often one sentence plus a pause. **The long-form answer is for learning; the short form is for the interview.** When you can give the long answer fluently, practise cutting it to its first sentence and stopping.
