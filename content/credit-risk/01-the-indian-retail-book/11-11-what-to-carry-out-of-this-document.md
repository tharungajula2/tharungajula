# §11 · WHAT TO CARRY OUT OF THIS DOCUMENT

Six things. If you retain nothing else:

1. **₹170.2 lakh crore, 688 million accounts, +16.6%** — the shape of the book, with home loans a third of the value and 3% of the accounts.
2. **The ticket-size gradient is monotone in every product** — and it has three possible causes, not one.
3. **Banks own value, NBFCs own volume** — and this is why bureau and RBI data disagree in direction.
4. **The November 2023 risk-weight exclusion list explains FY26's entire growth composition.** Housing, education, vehicle and gold were excluded, and those are exactly what grew.
5. **GNPA of 1.8% and personal loan PAR 180+ of 5.3% are both true** — the gap is universe, write-offs, denominator, definition and mix.
6. **Weekly bureau reporting went live on 1 July 2026**, and it will move your model monitoring before it moves your losses.
