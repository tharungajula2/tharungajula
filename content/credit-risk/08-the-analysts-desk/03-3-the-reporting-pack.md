# §3 · THE REPORTING PACK

Four standing artefacts. Each answers a different question for a different audience, and knowing which is which is most of the skill.

## 3.1 The origination pack — *what are we writing?*

Audience: business heads, credit policy, and whoever owns growth.

- Volume and value by product, channel, geography, ticket band — with month-on-month and year-on-year.
- **Approval rate**, and the decomposition into policy declines and score declines (Document 04 §5.1).
- **Score distribution of approvals**, against the development sample.
- **Deviation rate by approving authority** (Document 02 §6.1).
- Average ticket, tenor, LTV, FOIR.
- **PSI on the applicant population.**

📘 **This is the only pack that is genuinely forward-looking.** Everything else reports on business already written. Deviation rate and approval-rate drift move *before* delinquency does, which is exactly what makes them worth a senior person's attention.

## 3.2 The portfolio pack — *what do we hold?*

Audience: risk committee, CRO, finance.

- Outstanding by product, segment, vintage.
- Delinquency by bucket, **by value and by account**, with the **rupee numerator alongside every ratio** (Document 03 §2.2).
- **Transition matrix and terminal-roll implied NPA formation** (Document 03 §4.3).
- **Vintage curves**, quarterly refresh, cut by channel and score band.
- Stage distribution and provision — Stage 1 / 2 / 3, with movement analysis.
- Concentration: top exposures, geography, sector.

## 3.3 The collections pack — *are we recovering?*

Audience: collections head, operations, risk.

- Bucket balances and flow, per the identity in Document 03 §5.1.
- **Roll rate and cure rate by bucket** — and specifically the 1–30 to 31–60 roll.
- Resolution mix: cured, settled, written off, recovered.
- Productivity: contacts, right-party contacts, promises made, **promises kept**.
- Cost per rupee recovered by bucket and treatment.

## 3.4 The model monitoring pack — *are our models still valid?*

Audience: model risk, validation, the board's risk committee under the governance regime of Document 04's opening.

- **PSI on the score; CSI on every characteristic** (Document 03 §8.3).
- **Bad rate by score band on matured cohorts** — the rank-ordering check, which is the one to lead with.
- Gini and KS decay on the newest matured cohort.
- Override and deviation rates against model recommendations.
- Model inventory status, tier, last validation date, open findings.

## 3.5 The one-page version

From Document 03 §9, the five numbers for a credit committee. Worth memorising as a structure, because being able to answer *"if you could only show five numbers, which?"* is a genuine test:

1. **Gross flow into the 1–30 bucket**, with the terminal-roll conversion into expected NPA formation.
2. **The 1–30 to 31–60 roll rate**, by segment.
3. **MOB 6 cumulative 90+ for the three most recent mature cohorts**, by channel.
4. **Approval rate and deviation rate** by approving authority.
5. **PSI and CSI** on the live scorecard.

Two flow, one cohort, one origination discipline, one model validity. **Nothing on that list is a stock number**, and that is the point.
