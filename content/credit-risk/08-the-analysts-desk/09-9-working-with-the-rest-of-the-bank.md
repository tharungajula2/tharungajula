# §9 · WORKING WITH THE REST OF THE BANK

**Business.** They are not the enemy and they are not stupid. They are optimising volume under a target you did not set. **Bring them findings they can act on**, and bring them early — a partner switched off in month two is a smaller conversation than one switched off in month eight after ₹200 crore of bad business.

**Finance.** They own the number that goes to the market. Reconcile before you publish, always (§2.3). From 2027 they become your closest working relationship, because ECL sits in the P&L and the provision is jointly owned.

**IT and data engineering.** Every snapshot, every feed, every point-in-time table depends on them. **Being the risk analyst who can write the query, read the schema and specify the change precisely is worth more than any single model you will build** — and it is the clearest place your technical background compounds.

**Collections.** They will tell you what the data cannot: that a particular geography has become impossible to visit, that a partner's customers are unreachable, that a treatment has stopped working. **Field intelligence leads the data by weeks.** Spend time on the collections floor; almost nobody in analytics does, and it shows in their models.

**Internal audit and validation.** Adversarial by design, and correctly so. Make their job easy — documented, reproducible, reconciled — and they become an ally who finds your errors before a supervisor does.
