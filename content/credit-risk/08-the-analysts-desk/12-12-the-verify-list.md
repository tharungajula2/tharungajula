# §12 · THE [VERIFY] LIST

**Confirmed by this document:**

- ✅ **CIMS** is the RBI's returns platform, with statutory and supervisory returns migrated in phases through 2023–2025 and transition for various categories of regulated entity completed by May 2025; it also carries digital lending app reporting.
- ✅ **DAKSH** is the advanced supervisory monitoring system, carrying payment fraud reporting through CPFIR since January 2023.

**Still open, carried forward:**

- ⚠️ **Draft Guidance on Model Risk Management, 2026** — comments closed 24 July 2026, finalisation unconfirmed. §7.3's breadth-of-definition point depends on it.
- ⚠️ **Prudential floor percentages** under the ACPIR Directions, 2026; the **CET1 transitional add-back profile**; **LTV bucket boundaries** in the housing risk-weight bands; the **granularity criterion** for regulatory retail.
- ⚠️ Whether **RBI stress-testing or ICAAP guidance** has been reissued under the 2025–26 consolidation programme.
- The exact current name of the **NBFC Ind AS prudential floor** circular; **recovery-agent Second Amendment Directions**; **KYC updation backstop** of 30 June 2026; **ULI** rollout scale; **RDCL** issuance volumes; **SARFAESI** thresholds for NBFCs and HFCs.

**Opened by this document:**

- **The exact current CIMS return inventory** relevant to retail credit risk — which returns, at what frequency, on what definitions. This is institution-specific and changes; get the list from the team rather than from commentary.
