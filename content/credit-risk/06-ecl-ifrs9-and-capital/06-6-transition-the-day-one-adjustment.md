# §6 · TRANSITION — THE DAY-ONE ADJUSTMENT

## 6.1 The mechanism 

📘 **The transitional adjustment amount** is the difference between the **ECL required as at 1 April 2027** — computed on the balance sheet position at 31 March 2027 — and the **provisions held under the existing norms at 31 March 2027**.

That difference is **added back to Common Equity Tier 1 capital**, and the impact on profitability and capital adequacy may be **spread over a four-year period**, FY2027-28 through FY2030-31, ending **31 March 2031**.

`[VERIFY]` The exact year-by-year add-back profile — whether the unwind is straight-line at 25% a year or a declining schedule — I have not confirmed from the Directions. The four-year window and the CET1 add-back mechanism are confirmed; the profile is not.

## 6.2 Worked

🧮 A bank with **₹1,00,000 crore CET1** and **₹6,50,000 crore RWA** — a CET1 ratio of **15.38%**.

At 31 March 2027 it holds **₹8,000 crore** of provisions under the existing norms. Its computed ECL at 1 April 2027 is **₹11,000 crore**.

**Transitional adjustment = ₹3,000 crore.**

**Without the arrangement**, that ₹3,000 crore hits CET1 on day one. CET1 becomes ₹97,000 crore and the ratio falls to **14.92%** — a **46 basis point** drop overnight, before tax effects.

**With the arrangement**, the ₹3,000 crore is added back and unwound across four years, so roughly **₹750 crore a year**, or about **11–12 basis points annually** — absorbable out of retained earnings for a profitable bank.

## 6.3 Why the RBI did it, and what it does not do

The purpose is to prevent a **capital cliff**: a system-wide, single-day fall in capital ratios that would force simultaneous deleveraging across the banking system precisely because of an accounting change rather than any deterioration in credit.

⚠️ **But note what the transition does not do.** It smooths the **day-one** adjustment. It does nothing about **ongoing volatility** thereafter. From 1 April 2027 onward, provisions move with Stage 2 migration and with macro scenario updates — and Document 03's growth-dilution arithmetic and §2.4's Stage 2 cliff both feed directly into a P&L that was previously insulated by rule-based provisioning. **The structural change is not the day-one number; it is that provisions become model-driven and therefore volatile, permanently.**

🔴 That is the real answer to "what changes for a bank in April 2027." Not the level of provisions. **The variance.**
