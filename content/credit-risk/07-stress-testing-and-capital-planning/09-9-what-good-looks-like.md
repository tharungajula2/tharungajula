# §9 · WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

Six markers, and they double as questions to ask about a function you are considering joining.

1. **Scenarios are owned by the board**, not selected by the team that will be judged on the results.
2. **Reverse stress testing is done and its conclusions are acted on** — §5.2.
3. **Satellite model limitations are documented**, particularly the macro-to-default weakness of §4.1.
4. **Results reach the P&L, not only the capital ratio** — §4.3 showed a bank can be comfortably capital-adequate and still lose a year's earnings.
5. **The use test passes** — a business decision demonstrably changed. §6.3.
6. **Contingency actions are pre-agreed, sequenced and realistic** about availability in stressed markets.
