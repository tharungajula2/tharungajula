# §11 · WHAT TO CARRY OUT OF THIS DOCUMENT

1. **Behavioural beats application on the same population**, because past repayment on the same obligation is the best proxy available — but the populations differ, so the Ginis are not comparable.
2. **The strongest behavioural variables are about change, not level** — which is also why they map naturally onto SICR.
3. **Collections should optimise uplift, not propensity.** The top of a propensity ranking is largely accounts that would have cured anyway.
4. **Score is linear in log-odds and exponential in risk.** 80 points ≈ 15× the PD.
5. **Lifetime PD is built from declining marginal hazards**, not from multiplying the 12-month PD — 8.1% here, not 15%.
6. **LGD must be discounted and cost-net.** 30% versus 53% on identical cash flows.
7. **Model LGD as cure probability × severity given no cure**, because the distribution is bimodal.
8. **A distressed borrower draws down** — so PD, LGD and EAD all move adversely together, which is what makes revolving credit the most dangerous retail asset.
9. **EAD is the only parameter you directly control**, through limit management.
10. **ECL differs from Basel EL in five ways**, and shares only the three parameters.
11. **Where the prudential floor binds, the model is decorative** — know where that is before you spend effort there.
