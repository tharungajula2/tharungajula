## Why this document exists first

Most credit risk material fails an Indian practitioner in one of two ways. Either it teaches the global framework and bolts India on at the end, or it teaches an Indian regime that no longer exists.

Both failures have the same root: **no sourcing discipline.** So this set starts with the sources, and every claim in the eight documents that follow carries the instrument name and date that backs it.

### How everything in this set is tagged

Every regulatory statement carries a status tag. **Conflating these is the single most common way a candidate sounds wrong in an interview**, because it makes you appear to be describing a regime you haven't actually worked in.

| Tag | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| **[IN FORCE]** | Operative today, 29 July 2026 |
| **[FROM 01-04-2027]** | Notified, commencement date set, not yet operative |
| **[DRAFT]** | Issued for consultation, not final |
| **[VERIFY]** | I could not confirm this to a primary source — check before you use it |

And one convention that matters more than it sounds: **when I say "today," I mean the as-at date at the top of this document.** If you are reading this in 2028, the ACPIR Directions are in force and half the tags have flipped. Part V tells you how to re-check.
