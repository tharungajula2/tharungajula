When you stop focusing on a specific task, your brain does not turn off. It switches to the Default Mode Network (DMN).

This network is responsible for self-reflection, daydreaming, and simulating the future. It is where human creativity lives. But the DMN can only boot up when the brain is quiet. By constantly bombarding our minds with rapid notifications and feeds, we are actively suppressing the DMN. We are trading our ability to plan our long-term future for short-term stimulation.
