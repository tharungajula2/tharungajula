---
title: "Brain Drain (Proximity Effect)"
date: "2026-02-22"
status: "CONCEPT"
protocol: "3"
excerpt: "Why your IQ drops just by having a smartphone in your peripheral vision."
---

Your working memory is like computer RAM. It has a strict limit.

Studies (like Ward et al.) show that even if your phone is face down and silent, its mere presence on the desk drains your cognitive capacity. Why? Because your brain subconsciously knows the portal is there, and it has to actively burn RAM to suppress the urge to check it. This is called an "inhibition cost". To truly do deep work, the device must be in another room. Out of sight is the only way to free the RAM.
