City water is full of heavy metals, chlorine, and microplastics. Reverse Osmosis (RO) is the firewall that blocks them.

The problem is that RO is too aggressive. It strips out all the beneficial minerals - magnesium, calcium, and potassium. It creates "Dead Water". Drinking this long-term can actually pull minerals out of your own body to balance the pH. The fix is a remineralization stage post-RO. You have to filter the water, and then consciously rebuild it.
