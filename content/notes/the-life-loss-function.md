In Machine Learning, a neural network learns by calculating its "Loss Function" - the mathematical difference between its prediction and the actual truth. The algorithm uses Gradient Descent to adjust its weights and minimize that loss over time.

Human cognition should work the exact same way. A failure is not a permanent state; it is just a high loss value. The goal is not to avoid failure, but to calculate the error, adjust your mental model (the weights), and run the next iteration. If you stop iterating, the loss becomes permanent.
