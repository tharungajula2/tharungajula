We worry about what we eat, but we breathe 11,000 liters of air a day. 

In Tier 2/3 Indian cities, PM2.5 particles (particulate matter smaller than 2.5 microns) are a silent systemic threat. Because they are so small, they bypass the lungs entirely, enter the bloodstream, and can cross the blood-brain barrier. It causes chronic inflammation, which degrades cognitive focus and accelerates geriatric decline. Pressurizing the home with HEPA filtration is not a luxury; it is basic environmental defense.
