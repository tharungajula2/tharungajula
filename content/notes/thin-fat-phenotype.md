The Indian genetic baseline has a bug when exposed to the modern environment. 

You can have a normal BMI, thin arms, and thin legs, but store a highly dangerous amount of visceral fat (fat around the internal organs). This is the "Thin-Fat" phenotype. It means your insulin resistance is skyrocketing even if you don't look overweight. We have to debug the traditional diet, which has become heavily skewed toward refined carbohydrates, and replace it with a higher protein-to-energy ratio.
