Algorithms are not neutral. They are engineered to extract attention using variable reward schedules - the exact same mechanic as a slot machine.

In neuroscience, there is a gap between "Wanting" (craving) and "Liking" (actual pleasure). Variable rewards spike dopamine to create massive "Wanting", even if the "Liking" is zero. This is why you can scroll for two hours, feel completely numb, and still find it hard to stop. It is a hijacked feedback loop.
