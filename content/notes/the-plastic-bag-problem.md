---

If you walk into an Indian hospital, you will see patients carrying their entire medical history in a plastic bag. 

When a crisis hits, a doctor has roughly 3 minutes to diagnose the issue. If the data is fragmented across crumpled paper prescriptions from 5 different clinics, the doctor is operating with "Context Blindness." They treat the acute symptom, not the chronic system. We have to digitize and centralize family medical data to give doctors a compiled, searchable database.
