In a Tier 2/3 city in India, power grids are not perfectly stable. Summer temperatures can destroy the molecular structure of sensitive medications like insulin within hours.

We cannot rely on the city's infrastructure. We have to build a micro-cold chain inside the home. This means isolated thermal storage, battery backups for medical refrigerators, and strict temperature logging. If the medicine degrades before it enters the body, the biological protocol fails. The fortress must protect the supply lines.
