Homeostasis is the body's baseline state of balance. Allostasis is the work the body has to do to maintain that balance under stress.

If you have a bad night of sleep, an argument at work, and a highly processed meal, your body manages it. But it pays a physiological cost to do so. This cumulative cost is your Allostatic Load. It is like running a server at 99% CPU capacity for months. Eventually, the hardware burns out. You cannot trick the system; you have to actively discharge the load.
