# The Premise
The legacy education system teaches students to reason by analogy—doing things because "that's how it's always been done" or copying the person next to them. This leads to fragile knowledge. When a novel problem appears, the student panics because there is no analogy to copy.

# The First-Principles Breakdown
First Principles means boiling a situation down to its raw atoms. If a smartphone costs 80,000 rupees, you don't accept that price because "it's a famous brand." You break it down: glass, silicon, lithium. The raw materials cost 2,000 rupees. The rest is software, manufacturing, and marketing margins. The world is no longer magic; it is just a series of solvable equations.

# Curiosity OS Application
We train students to attack novel problems by isolating the undeniable facts. If an exam question looks foreign, they don't freeze. They isolate the variables they *do* know and build a completely custom solution from the ground up.
