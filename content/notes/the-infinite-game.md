In finite games (like sports or exams), the rules are fixed, and the goal is to win and end the game. 

Health, Family, and Cognition are Infinite Games. The objective is not to "win", but to keep playing. The moment you think you have optimized the system, entropy introduces a new variable - aging, a new disease, a new stressor. The purpose of this Lab is not to reach a static state of perfection. It is to build an engine that can continuously adapt to the chaos.
