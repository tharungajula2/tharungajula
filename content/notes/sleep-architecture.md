If you treat sleep like a biological off-switch, you will wake up broken. Sleep is an active, heavily structured process. 

Deep Sleep (Slow Wave) is the hardware maintenance phase. It clears physical waste (amyloid plaques) from the brain and repairs muscle tissue. REM Sleep is the software update. It consolidates memories, deletes emotional junk, and defragments the hard drive. You can be unconscious for 8 hours but still lack the proper "architecture" if your temperature or light exposure is wrong.
