title: "Glucose Area Under Curve"
date: "2026-02-26"
status: "CONCEPT"
protocol: "1"
excerpt: "Why a sharp metabolic spike is less dangerous than a prolonged, elevated plateau."
---

When looking at blood sugar data, people panic over a high peak. But the peak is not the real enemy. The enemy is the total time spent elevated.

In calculus, this is the Area Under the Curve (AUC). A massive spike that comes crashing back down to baseline in 45 minutes represents a healthy, highly responsive insulin system. A moderate spike that takes 4 hours to clear means the system is severely insulin resistant. The goal is not just to flatten the curve, but to compress the total time the pancreas has to work.
