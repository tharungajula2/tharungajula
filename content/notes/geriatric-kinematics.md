For a 60-year-old with severe knee pain, gravity is an active threat. 

The primary vector of catastrophic decline in the elderly is a physical fall. Preventing this requires engineering the kinematics of the home. This means high-contrast tape on stairs, removing loose rugs (trip hazards), installing grab bars at critical pivot points, and ensuring motion-activated floor lighting at night. We must change the environment so the organism does not have to adapt.
