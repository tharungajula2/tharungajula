---
title: "Telemetry vs. Pathology"
date: "2026-03-05"
status: "CONCEPT"
protocol: "1"
excerpt: "The difference between 'normal' blood work and optimal biological telemetry."
---

Standard medical reference ranges are based on the statistical average of the population. But the average population is metabolically sick.

If your fasting glucose is 99 mg/dL, a standard doctor will say you are "normal" because you have not yet crossed the threshold into the pathology of diabetes. But from a systems engineering perspective, 99 mg/dL is terrible telemetry. It means the engine is running hot. We must shift our standard from "not currently dying" to "operating at optimal parameters."
