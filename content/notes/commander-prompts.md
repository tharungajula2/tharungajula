# The Premise
Students currently use LLMs (like ChatGPT or Gemini) as cheat codes. They paste a homework prompt and copy the output so they can sleep. This guarantees they will be replaced by the very machine they are using.

# The First-Principles Breakdown
An AI should act as an exoskeleton for the human mind, not a replacement. A "Commander Prompt" forces the AI to act adversarially. Instead of asking for the answer, the user command the AI to ask *them* hard questions, point out logical flaws in their reasoning, and force them to synthesize a better architecture.

# Curiosity OS Application
We forbid "generate this for me" prompts. We grade students on the quality of their prompts. The student's value is no longer in writing the baseline code, but in how fast they can take AI-generated hints, filter out hallucinations, and orchestrate a flawless final product.
