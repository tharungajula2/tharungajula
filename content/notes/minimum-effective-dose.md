date: "2026-03-03"

protocol: "1"
excerpt: "The engineering principle of applying exactly enough stress to force an adaptation, and no more."
---

In pharmacology, the Minimum Effective Dose (MED) is the lowest dose of a drug required to produce the desired outcome. Anything beyond that just increases toxicity.

This law applies to physical training. Exercise is just a biological stressor that triggers an adaptation response. If it takes 3 sets of heavy squats to trigger muscle growth, doing 6 sets does not give you double the growth. It just gives you double the systemic fatigue. We must aim to find the precise threshold that flips the biological switch, and then immediately prioritize recovery.
