date: "2026-03-02"

excerpt: "Why eating the exact same meal at 10 PM causes twice the metabolic damage as 10 AM."
---

The digestive system does not have the same processing power at all hours of the day. It is ruled by the circadian clock.

At 10 AM, insulin sensitivity is at its absolute peak. The biological servers are fully online to process incoming glucose. By 10 PM, the system is powering down to prepare for cellular repair. If you eat a heavy meal during this "Dead Zone", the pancreas has to force the system back online. The result is massive glucose spikes and disrupted sleep architecture. Timing is just as important as the payload.
