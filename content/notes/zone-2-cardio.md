Most people train in the "grey zone" - too hard to build endurance, but too easy to build max power. 

Zone 2 is strictly exercising at a pace where you can comfortably hold a conversation (around 60-70% max heart rate). From a first-principles view, staying in this exact zone forces the body to use fat as its primary fuel source. More importantly, it stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis. You are literally telling your cells to build more engines.
