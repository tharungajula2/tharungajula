Your nervous system has a gas pedal (Sympathetic) and a brake pedal (Parasympathetic). The Vagus nerve is the physical cable that connects the brake pedal to your heart, lungs, and gut.

Most people in the modern world are driving with their foot permanently glued to the gas pedal. This creates chronic inflammation. You can manually engage the Vagus nerve through deep diaphragmatic breathing (which mechanically massages the nerve) or cold exposure. When you stimulate the Vagus nerve, you are forcing the biological software to terminate the stress response.
