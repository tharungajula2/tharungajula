---

When a geriatric emergency happens - a fall, a sudden spike in blood sugar, or a stroke - the default human reaction is panic. Panic burns the "Golden Hour" of response time.

Crisis Architecture means pre-computing your decisions. It means having a literal decision tree taped to the fridge or saved in the Family OS. If X happens, call Y, and give them Z data. You remove emotion from the logistics of saving a life.
