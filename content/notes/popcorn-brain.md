Neuroplasticity is just the brain wiring itself to be efficient at whatever you do most. 

If you spend 4 hours a day watching 15-second clips, your brain optimizes for rapid context switching. It strengthens those circuits. But when you put the phone down and try to read a book, or listen to a friend, reality feels painfully slow. Your brain interprets the lack of rapid stimulation as an error. You haven't lost your attention span; you have just trained it for a predatory environment.
