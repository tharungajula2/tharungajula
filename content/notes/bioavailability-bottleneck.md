Ingestion does not equal absorption. 

You can eat a nutrient-dense Indian meal, but if your gut lining is inflamed, the nutrients will pass right through the tube and exit the system. This is the bioavailability bottleneck. Certain compounds require specific conditions to be unlocked. Turmeric needs black pepper and fat to cross the intestinal wall. Iron from plants needs Vitamin C. You have to engineer the chemical pairing, otherwise, you are just creating expensive waste.
