# The Premise
Teenagers are treating their smartphones like digital pacifiers. The moment they feel slight friction or boredom, they reach for short-form video. This constant dopamine spiking destroys their attention spans and creates massive underlying anxiety.

# The First-Principles Breakdown
When the brain is constantly fed high-dopamine inputs, it downregulates its own receptors. Normal, difficult tasks (like reading or deep thinking) suddenly feel physically painful. Conversely, "Boredom" is actually the activation of the Default Mode Network (DMN), where the brain does background processing and connects distant, unrelated dots.

# Curiosity OS Application
We must explicitly teach students the neurobiology of their own attention. By explaining that tech algorithms are designed by psychologists to hack their brains, we trigger teenage rebellion against the machines. We institute "Boredom Incubation" periods to let their DMN synthesize what they've learned.
