---

Think of a mitochondrion as a car engine. Usually, it burns fuel (food) to create forward motion (ATP energy). 

But sometimes, the engine "uncouples". Instead of using the fuel to make ATP, it just burns the fuel and releases it as pure heat. In nature, this is how hibernating animals stay warm. For us, mild uncoupling (triggered by things like cold exposure or specific polyphenols) forces the body to burn more fat just to maintain baseline operations. It is a biological inefficiency that we can weaponize for metabolic health.
