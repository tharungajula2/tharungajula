In software engineering, when an application crashes, you look at the server logs. In human biology, the Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) is the server log.

For decades, we guessed how food impacted us. A CGM measures interstitial glucose every 5 minutes. It turns a black box into a transparent dashboard. You can literally watch a bowl of white rice spike the system, and watch a 10-minute post-meal walk flatten the curve. It is the ultimate tool for debugging metabolic syndrome.
