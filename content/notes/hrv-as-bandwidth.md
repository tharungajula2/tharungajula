A high Heart Rate is not the enemy; a rigid Heart Rate is. 

Heart Rate Variability (HRV) measures the micro-milliseconds of difference between your heartbeats. If the beats are perfectly even like a metronome, your nervous system is stressed and rigid. If there is high variability, your system is relaxed and highly responsive. Think of HRV as your biological WiFi bandwidth. When bandwidth is low, the system lags. To increase the bandwidth, you have to prioritize recovery over output.
