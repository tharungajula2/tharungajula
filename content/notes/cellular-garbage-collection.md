excerpt: "Autophagy is the biological equivalent of running a hard drive defragmentation."
---

Over time, your cells accumulate junk proteins and damaged organelles. If left unchecked, this cellular debris causes the system to lag and eventually crash (disease).

Autophagy is the body's built-in garbage collection program. It literally means "self-eating". When the body goes into a fasted state, it realizes no new fuel is coming in. So, it starts scavenging itself for parts, breaking down the damaged junk proteins and recycling them into fresh energy. You have to routinely starve the engine just a little bit to force it to clean itself.
