We view muscle strictly as a mechanical pulley system for lifting things. This is a flawed mental model. Muscle is actually an endocrine gland. 

When you contract skeletal muscle under heavy loads, it secretes specialized hormones called myokines. These molecules travel through the bloodstream and signal the brain to improve cognitive function, signal the bones to increase density, and signal fat tissue to release energy. Muscle acts as a massive metabolic sink that regulates the entire organism.
