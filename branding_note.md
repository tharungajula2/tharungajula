### 🏛️ PART 1: THE BRAND DNA (The Rules)

Before we open PowerPoint or Canva, we need to define your "Brand Kit." These are the strict rules you must follow to keep everything looking like the website.

#### 1. The Color Palette (Dark Mode Only)

Your brand is **95% Black/Grey** and **5% Neon Accents**. Never use white backgrounds.

* **Background (The Void):** `#000000` (Pure Black) or `#09090b` (Zinc-950 - The website background).
* **Text (Primary):** `#e4e4e7` (Zinc-200 - Almost white, but softer).
* **Text (Secondary):** `#a1a1aa` (Zinc-400 - Muted grey for descriptions).
* **The Trinity Accents (Use sparingly for focus):**
* **Protocol N=1 (Bio):** `#10b981` (Emerald-500).
* **Protocol Family (Home):** `#f97316` (Orange-500).
* **Protocol Clinical (Ops):** `#3b82f6` (Blue-500).



#### 2. The Typography (Fonts)

* **Headings (The "Code" Look):** Use **"Geist Mono"**, **"JetBrains Mono"**, or **"Courier New"** (Bold). If those aren't available, use **"Inter"** (Extra Bold) with tight spacing.
* **Body Text (Readability):** Use **"Inter"** or **"Arial"** (Regular). Keep it clean.

#### 3. The Visual Elements

* **Glassmorphism:** Slight transparency with a thin border. (e.g., Black box with 50% opacity and a thin white/grey border).
* **Glows:** Soft, blurred circles behind key elements (like the hero section of your site).
* **Grids:** Very faint background grids to give that "Engineering/Blueprint" feel.

### 🖥️ PART 2: THE POWERPOINT / KEYNOTE TEMPLATE

We will create a "Master Slide" that you can use forever.

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

1. **Background:** Set the slide background to **Pure Black (`#000000`)**.
2. **The Grid (Crucial):**
* Find a "Grid Pattern" PNG (transparent background) on Google.
* Overlay it on the black background.
* Set transparency to **5%** (It should be barely visible). This creates the "Lab" feel.


3. **The Header (Top of Slide):**
* Add a small text box in the top-right corner: `// THARUN_HEALTH_LAB`.
* Font: Monospace (Courier/JetBrains), Size 12, Color: Dark Grey (`#52525b`).


4. **The Title Slide Layout:**
* **Big Title:** Center, White, Bold, Font Size 60.
* **Subtitle:** Center, Grey (`#a1a1aa`), Font Size 24.
* **The "Glow":** In PowerPoint, insert a Circle shape behind the text. Fill it with your accent color (e.g., Emerald Green). Go to **Effects > Soft Edges** and crank it up to 50pt or 100pt until it looks like a diffused light.


5. **The Content Slide Layout:**
* **Title:** Top Left, White, Size 32.
* **Accent Line:** Add a thin horizontal line under the title in one of your Trinity colors (Green, Orange, or Blue).
* **Body:** Left aligned, Grey text.



**Pro Tip:** Never use bullet points like "•". Use custom bullets like `>` or `//` or `01.` to match your coding aesthetic.
