# §5 · REVERSE STRESS TESTING

## 5.1 What it is

📘 Conventional stress testing starts with a scenario and computes the outcome. **Reverse stress testing starts with the outcome — the bank becomes non-viable — and works backwards to find the scenarios that produce it.** Then it asks: how plausible is that, and what would we see coming?

## 5.2 Why it is the most valuable exercise and the least done

🔴 **Conventional stress testing has a built-in bias toward comfortable answers**, and the mechanism is worth being explicit about. The scenarios are chosen by the institution being tested. Nobody proposes a scenario that breaks their own bank, because doing so invites a supervisory conversation nobody wants. So severity is calibrated, consciously or not, to a level the institution survives — and the exercise then confirms resilience, which is what everyone expected.

**Reverse stress testing removes that degree of freedom by fixing the outcome.** You cannot calibrate your way out of it. The output is a set of scenarios that break you, and the discussion becomes about their plausibility and about early warning indicators — which is a far more useful conversation than "we passed."

✅ **The three outputs a good reverse stress test produces:**

1. **The binding constraint.** Which exposure, concentration or funding dependency breaks first. Often not the one management expected.
2. **The early indicators.** If that scenario were beginning, what would move first? Those become monitoring triggers.
3. **The contingency actions**, pre-agreed and sequenced, with a realistic assessment of how long each takes and whether it is available in a stressed market — because capital raising is hardest precisely when it is most needed.

⚠️ **And the honest observation about how it goes in practice.** The output of a reverse stress test is uncomfortable by construction, which is why the exercise is frequently done as a formality and its conclusions rarely change anything. **Whether an institution acts on its reverse stress test is one of the better available indicators of whether its risk function has real standing.**
