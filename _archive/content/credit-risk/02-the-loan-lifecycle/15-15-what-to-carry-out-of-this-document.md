# §15 · WHAT TO CARRY OUT OF THIS DOCUMENT

1. **Ten stages, and the upstream principle** — cost of fixing rises by an order of magnitude at each stage, so almost every collections problem is an underwriting problem that arrived late.
2. **Underwriting is four decisions, kept separate**: policy, bureau, affordability, score. And the score ranks — the cut-off decides.
3. **Deviation rate by approving authority** is the earliest signal that discipline is slipping, because it appears at origination.
4. **SMA runs at day-end, at borrower level, on FIFO** — and you can walk the calendar from due date to NPA.
5. **An NPA upgrades only on entire arrears** — which is why deep buckets in unsecured retail are sticky.
6. **Technical write-off removes the asset, not the liability** — and it is the main reason GNPA and bureau PAR diverge.
7. **The target variable is always generated downstream of the decision being modelled** — so you are always modelling the past, on a censored sample, with degradation you cannot see until it is expensive.
