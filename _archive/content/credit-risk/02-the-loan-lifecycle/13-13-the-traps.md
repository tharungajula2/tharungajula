# §13 · THE TRAPS

1. **Treating a collections problem as a collections problem.** Cut by vintage and channel first. §1.
2. **Blending policy rejections into scorecard performance.** They are different decisions and mixing them destroys both the cut-off analysis and the model's Gini. §5.1.
3. **Confusing V-CIP with OTP-based e-KYC.** One is face-to-face equivalent; one carries limits. §3.2.
4. **Describing fraud classification as an internal determination.** Natural justice, show-cause, hearing — *SBI v. Rajesh Agarwal*. §4.2.
5. **Leaving first-party fraud in a PD development sample.** §4.1.
6. **Quoting a single FOIR cap.** It is a schedule against income, for arithmetic reasons. §5.3.
7. **Saying "penal interest".** It is a penal charge, non-capitalised, since 1 April 2024. §6.3.
8. **Treating "secured" as one LGD.** Pledge, hypothecation and mortgage are three different loss profiles. §7.2.
9. **Saying an NPA upgrades when DPD falls below 90.** It upgrades only on payment of entire arrears. §11.2.
10. **Reading an improving GNPA without the write-off number.** §11.4.
11. **Citing the Fair Practices Code circular** for something now consolidated into the Responsible Business Conduct Directions, 2025. §2.3.
12. **Stating the draft recovery-agent amendments as live.** They proposed 1 July 2026; finalisation unconfirmed. §10.4.
