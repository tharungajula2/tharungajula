## HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

Document 01 was a photograph: what exists, held by whom, at what stress level. This document is the film. It follows one loan through ten stages, and at each stage asks the same four questions:

1. **What decision is being made here?**
2. **What is the control point** — the thing that, if it fails, causes loss?
3. **What rule governs it**, and is that rule current?
4. **What data does this stage generate**, and what model sits on that data?

That fourth question is why this document matters more to you than to most people learning credit risk. **Every model in credit risk is a model of a decision that happens at a specific point in this lifecycle.** An application scorecard exists because of Stage 4. A behavioural scorecard exists because of Stage 8. A collection scorecard exists because of Stage 9. PD, LGD and EAD are estimates about Stages 9, 10 and 11. If you know the lifecycle, the models stop being a list of techniques and become a set of answers to questions the business is actually asking.

> **The one sentence that organises this document**
>
> A loan does not go bad at the moment of default. It goes bad at the moment of a decision — and the entire discipline of credit risk is the practice of finding **which decision**, **how far upstream**, and **whether it was the policy, the model, the channel, or the execution** that failed.
