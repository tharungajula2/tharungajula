# §13 · THE CLOSING NOTE

You now have eight documents of substance and one of interrogation. A few honest things about what that does and doesn't give you.

**What it gives you.** A defensible, current, quantified account of the Indian retail credit market and the frameworks over it — with dates, with sources, and with the open questions marked rather than papered over. Most candidates in this market have the vocabulary. Far fewer have the arithmetic, and almost none have the arithmetic **attached to the right instrument on the right date**.

**What it doesn't.** It won't make you someone who has run a portfolio through a cycle. That happens the first time a number you published moves and you have to explain why in a room where it costs somebody something. Reading is preparation; the artefacts are evidence; the seat is the qualification.

**The thing that will age fastest.** Every `[VERIFY]` and `[DRAFT]` tag in this set. The model risk guidance, the recovery agent amendments, the prudential floor percentages, the LTV bucket boundaries. **Check them before you use them.** And when something in here turns out to be wrong — it will — that's the finding. Bring it back and we'll fix the document.

**And the one habit underneath all of it**, which outlasts every rule in these pages: *whose book, which date, which definition, and does it net off write-offs.* Four questions, ten seconds. They will catch most of the errors that make a good analyst look careless, and asking them out loud in a room is itself the signal.

Go and use it.
