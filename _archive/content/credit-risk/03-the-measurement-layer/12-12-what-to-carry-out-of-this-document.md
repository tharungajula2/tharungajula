# §12 · WHAT TO CARRY OUT OF THIS DOCUMENT

1. **Three views — cohort, flow, stock.** Name which one you are using. Only the first two can diagnose.
2. **Growth dilutes delinquency.** 4.0% becomes 2.7% with nothing improved. Every ratio in a fast-growing book is flattered.
3. **Vintage: level, shape, maturity point** — and always cut by sourcing partner.
4. **The terminal roll turns one month of data into a forward loss number**, and a 10-point move in the first roll cell moves annual NPA formation by nearly 2 points.
5. **The bucket identity forces the write-off question into the open** — which is what actually explains Document 01's stuck deep buckets.
6. **Same PD, four LGDs** — pledge, hypothecation, mortgage, unsecured.
7. **Segmentation is now regulatory**, and the test for a real segment is a different curve *shape*, not a different level.
8. **PSI without CSI is not stability monitoring** — and the 1 July 2026 bureau cadence change will make that distinction expensive for someone this year.
