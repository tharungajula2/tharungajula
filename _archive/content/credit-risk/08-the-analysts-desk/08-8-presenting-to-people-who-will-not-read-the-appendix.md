# §8 · PRESENTING TO PEOPLE WHO WILL NOT READ THE APPENDIX

## 8.1 Three rules

**One — inverted pyramid.** Conclusion first, then support, then method. Academic order is exactly backwards for this audience. If the room reads only your first line, it should still get the finding.

**Two — one chart, one message.** If you cannot write the message as a sentence in the chart title, the chart is not ready. *"PAR 31–90 by origination cohort"* is a label. *"Cohorts written after September are deteriorating at twice the rate at the same age"* is a message.

**Three — the number that matters, not the number that is interesting.** Gini went from 0.38 to 0.44 is interesting to you. **The swap set is worth ₹X crore of avoided loss at constant approval rate** is what matters to them (Document 04 §11.2).

## 8.2 Presenting uncertainty without becoming useless

You will often have to say something with weak evidence — Document 05 §5.5 on LGD, Document 06 §3.3 on macro relationships.

🔴 **The failure mode is hedging until the statement carries no information.** *"It could go either way depending on assumptions"* is technically true and operationally worthless.

✅ **The structure that works: a point estimate, a range, a driver, and a trigger.**

> *"Central case is 3.2%. Plausible range 2.6% to 4.5%, and the width is driven almost entirely by LGD, where we have 140 completed workouts. If the 1–30 roll rate exceeds 3% for two consecutive months, I would move to the upper half of that range."*

That is honest about uncertainty **and** decision-useful, and it tells the room exactly what to watch. It also protects you: you named the trigger in advance.

## 8.3 The vocabulary problem

Different words for different rooms, same underlying fact:

| To a modeller | To a business head | To a board |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Gini fell from 0.44 to 0.36 OOT | The scorecard is separating good from bad much less well than when we built it | Our lending decisions are becoming less accurate |
| PSI 0.31 on the score distribution | The applicants coming through the door are materially different from the ones the model was built on | We are using a tool built for a different market |
| Terminal roll 22.3% at 2.5% inflow | About one in four rupees entering early delinquency ends up as an NPA | Roughly ₹190 crore of additional annual NPA formation |

⚠️ **This is translation, not simplification, and the distinction matters.** Nothing in the right-hand column is less precise than the left; it is precise about a different thing — the consequence rather than the mechanism. **An analyst who cannot do this stays in the analyst chair**, because every promotion in this field is a promotion into rooms that care about consequences.
