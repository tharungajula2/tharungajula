# §10 · THE TRAPS

1. **Treating SICR as an absolute risk test.** It is relative to origination — which is why origination PDs must be retained. §2.3.
2. **Confusing Stage 2→1 with Stage 3→1.** The first is a model determination; the second requires entire arrears. §2.3.
3. **Multiplying a 12-month PD by tenor** for lifetime. Document 05 §4.4.
4. **Running ECL on a central macro scenario.** Convexity means the weighted average of ECLs exceeds the ECL of the average scenario. §3.2.
5. **Presenting a fitted macro-to-default relationship for Indian retail** as robust. §3.3.
6. **Letting an overlay persist unchanged across many quarters.** It has become an unacknowledged model deficiency. §3.4.
7. **Merging the two glide paths.** EIR to 31 March 2030; provisioning smoothing to 31 March 2031. §4.2.
8. **Ignoring EIR's income-recognition effect** — processing fees stop being day-one income. §4.3.
9. **Investing modelling effort where the prudential floor binds.** §5.2.
10. **Pricing off reported provisions** in floored segments rather than off modelled EL. §5.2.
11. **Thinking the transition arrangement solves volatility.** It smooths day one only. §6.3.
12. **Assuming PD and LGD models drive Indian regulatory capital.** India is SA-only. §1, §7.1.
13. **Forgetting that housing risk weight depends on system-wide loan count** — a bureau lookup, not an internal field. §7.4.
14. **Treating a provision increase as capital-neutral** because RWA also falls. The numerator effect dominates roughly 14 to 1 in the worked case. §8.1.
15. **Listing procyclicality as a criticism without naming the dampeners** — transition, floors, and SA capital. §8.2.
