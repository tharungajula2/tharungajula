## Monday, 9:40 a.m.

There are seven people in the room and a slide on the screen that says **"AI-Powered Underwriting Transformation."** The salesperson who made that slide is not in the room. He closed the deal in March and is now working on a hospital system in Ohio.

Priya Raghavan, VP of Lending, opens with: *"So how long until this is making decisions?"*

Dan Whitfield, the Chief Credit Officer, has not touched his coffee. He says: *"Before that — can it tell me why it declined someone?"*

Rina Castellanos, Security, asks: *"Where does the data go?"*

Three questions. Every one of them is a different discipline. If you answer any of them wrong in the first week, you spend the next six months digging out.

Here is the discipline that separates FDEs from consultants: **you do not answer yet.** You say, honestly, *"Before I give you numbers I want to watch someone actually do the job. Can I sit with an underwriter for two days?"* Priya will find this underwhelming. Dan will find it the first reassuring thing anyone from your company has said.

You spend the week doing two things at once: learning what Meridian actually does, and making sure you understand — precisely, mechanically, without hand-waving — the machine you are proposing to put inside their building. This document is the second thing. The first thing happens in the margins, and by Friday it changes the whole project.
