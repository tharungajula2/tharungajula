## How to use this

The other eight documents teach. **This one checks.** Different job, different mechanics.

**Part I — Fast recall.** Question in bold, answer right after. Cover the answer, say yours out loud, then look. Out loud matters — recognising an answer and being able to produce one are different capabilities, and only the second survives a meeting.

**Part II — The trap index.** Error message, cause, fix. Scan it monthly.

**Part III — The numbers.** Facts you should never have to look up.

**Part IV — Say it out loud.** The standard questions you will be asked in rooms, with answers timed at roughly ninety seconds. Rehearse these until they're boring.

**Part V — Judgement drills.** Scenarios where the answer is a tradeoff, not a fact. There is no single right answer; there is reasoning that holds up and reasoning that doesn't.

**Part VI — Hostile questioning.** The hard ones, asked the way a skeptic asks them.

**The honest instruction: don't read this document front to back.** Pick a section, spend fifteen minutes, close it. It's built for the fiftieth pass, not the first.
