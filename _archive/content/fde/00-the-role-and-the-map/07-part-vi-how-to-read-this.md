## PART VI — HOW TO READ THIS

**Order is not optional.** Each document opens mid-deployment and assumes the previous one. Skipping ahead breaks the narrative that makes it stick.

**Daily rhythm that actually works:** one sitting on new material, one shorter sitting re-reading something from two documents back. New material teaches; re-reading is what converts it into something you can retrieve under pressure in a meeting. Most people do only the first and wonder why nothing holds.

**Read the traces even when you're sure.** The trace is where the misunderstanding you didn't know you had surfaces. Skipping traces because the code "looks obvious" is the most common way to accumulate silent gaps.

**When you have a machine, chase the `[RECEIPT]` markers first.** Not every exercise. The receipts.

**When you lose the thread, come back to this document.** Re-read Part II and Part III. The deployment arc and the five people at Meridian are the skeleton everything else hangs on.
