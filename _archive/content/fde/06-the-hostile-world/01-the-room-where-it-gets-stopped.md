## The room where it gets stopped

Rina Castellanos is there, and so is someone you haven't met: Adaeze Nwosu, fair lending counsel, who joined because the words "AI" and "underwriting" appeared in the same change request.

Rina's question is: *what can this thing be made to do that we don't want?*

Adaeze's question is worse, and it is the one that can end the project: *can you prove this doesn't discriminate?*

Notice that neither question is about accuracy. You have sixty days of eval data and it buys you nothing here. **A system can be 94% accurate and completely unshippable**, and the reason is that accuracy is a property of the system's outputs while both of these questions are about the system's *structure* — what it's capable of, and what its errors do to different groups of people.

Which is the thesis of this entire document: **security and fairness are architecture, not vigilance.** You do not defend a system by being careful with it. You defend it by building one that cannot do the thing.
